TO DEVELOP BEFORE SCHEMATIC DESIGN
Survey + permissions. Confirm legal boundary, bearings, road width/reservation, approved land use, setbacks,
floor-area/height controls and levels along the entire frontage. The drawn envelope is a feasibility assumption,
not a zero-setback approval.
Frame + ground. Coordinate geotechnical advice, excavation support beside the raised road, retaining walls,
foundations, seismic frame behaviour and parking waterproofing. Columns shown in the plans are indicative only.
Services + demand. Confirm water, sewer/outfall, power, drainage, mechanical ventilation and metering. Check
actual tenant demand and short-stay permissions before fixing fit-outs or operating assumptions.
